The problem

Most agencies land on Looker Studio because it is free, then find out what it costs. It is native to Google and nothing else, so every non-Google platform needs a paid connector. It puts a vendor footer on work you are charging a client for. Nothing sends itself. And every new client means building and then maintaining another report by hand.

What it does

  • The whole client roster on one screen: health, leads, cost per lead, spend pacing against budget.
  • Alerts you before the client notices — a dropped lead source, a slipping response time, spend running ahead of budget.
  • Budget recommendations with the maths attached.
  • Client-ready reports in one click, or scheduled weekly or monthly and sent automatically.
  • Role-based access: the owner sees margin, account managers do not, clients get a read-only view.
  • White label throughout — console, reports, scheduled email and the client login all carry your agency.
